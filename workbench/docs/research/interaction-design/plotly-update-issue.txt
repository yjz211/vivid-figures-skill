The plot/axis titles can be conveniently edited 'inline', i.e. in the same place that they're displayed, but this doesn't trigger the
PlotlyEditor.onUpdate
callback. On the other hand, they sync nicely with the editors in the controls sidebar under Style > General/Axes, and making the edits there does correctly call
onUpdate
.
For my application this makes the inline editing effectively broken, as the changes won't be saved. Is there a way to disable it so that users don't get confused?
Overall though this is a great project that'll be very valuable to us, so thank you!
